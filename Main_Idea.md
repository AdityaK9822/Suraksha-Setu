How to Tackle using Block-chain:

1. User creates an account on our portal
2. User can Join community orgs or Create orgs as admin, or store his private docs/assests
3. the uploaded Document is Hashed and Stored in a blockchain. 
4. Community documents need to verified by the admins or authorized people



Hashing Algo: Keccak-256 (EVM)
