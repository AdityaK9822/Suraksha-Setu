01 — THE PROBLEM

Property records shouldn’t be difficult to find.

iProperty documents are scattered across physical files and digital folders.
iiHomeowners struggle to organise important records.
iiiHousing societies manage fragmented member information.
ivBanks need authorised access to relevant documents.
vSharing documents without clear consent creates privacy concerns.
02 — THE SOLUTION

One connection. Three stakeholders. Greater trust.

S

Housing Societies

Register the community and approve who may ask.
H

Homeowners

Organise records and give consent, document by document.
B

Banks

Request specific documents for a stated purpose.
03 — HOW IT WORKS

Four steps. Every one on record.

Society registers its community

Buildings, flats and office bearers are set up once, creating a trusted directory.

Homeowners organise their records

Property, loan and society documents are uploaded and categorised in one workspace.

Banks request specific documents

A request names the documents, the purpose and an expiry date.

Society authorisation and homeowner consent

Access opens only when both approve, and only for the time requested.

04 — PRIVACY AND CONTROL

Your documents. Your permission.

Specific requests

Banks ask for named documents, never blanket access.

Two approvals

Society approval and homeowner consent are separate steps.

Time-limited

Every grant carries an expiry and then closes.

Transparent history

Each request, approval and view is logged for everyone involved.

Future control

Homeowners can revoke access whenever they choose.
